How it works

A retrofit, not a replacement.

A new smart machine runs thousands. VendSmart adds the same intelligence to the machines you already own.
STEP 01

Sensors inside

Low-cost IR sensors mount in the machine and detect when each slot runs empty. No rewiring the unit.

→
STEP 02

A small board reports

An Arduino controller reads the sensors and pushes stock status up to the cloud over the machine's connection.

→
STEP 03

You see it live

The app updates in real time. Alerts fire the moment a slot empties, before you've lost a day of sales.
